How is the score calculated?

Each market gets a score from 0–100 relative to the other 9 candidate markets (not an absolute benchmark), combining three equally-weighted pillars: Analytical (40% import-demand growth + 30% tariff burden, inverted + 30% governance/institutional-risk composite), Strategic (50% demand growth + 50% market headroom — the inverse of Ecuador's current penetration), and Business/investment perception (30% governance stability + 20% FX stability + 25% logistics performance + 25% transit time, inverted). This is the same methodology used in the full dashboard's "Should Ecuador Export Here?" section — see data/SOURCES.md for every underlying figure and citation, and known data gaps (e.g. a few tariff lines for Korea/China/Japan instant coffee remain unconfirmed, 2022 LPI global ranks are not yet published by the World Bank, and real freight cost is intentionally omitted for lack of a reliable free source).
