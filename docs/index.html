How to read this dashboard

  1. Pick a coffee type in the menu above (green, roasted, instant, etc.) — almost the whole dashboard recalculates based on your selection.
  2. Go through the 5 chapters in order: overview → competition & prices → market access → risk & logistics → final conclusion.
  3. Each purple box ("Key takeaway") gives you the conclusion in one sentence. If you want the technical detail and sources, click "See methodology & sources".
  4. Some sections (competitors, world prices, agricultural production, country risk, logistics) use data that isn't broken down by coffee type — each case explains why it doesn't change.
