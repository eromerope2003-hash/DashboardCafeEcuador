The Real Story: Raw Beans vs. Instant Coffee Extract

HS 0901 (green/roasted beans) vs. HS 210111 (soluble/instant coffee extract) export value.

Ecuador's coffee export value is not where you'd expect it. In 2024, instant/soluble coffee extract (HS 210111) exported $122.1M — roughly 10x the value of raw green beans ($12.2M). This means "local coffee firms" is really two very different populations: smallholder growers exposed to weather and land-use pressure, and a handful of large industrial processors exposed to global commodity and trade-policy risk.

Ecuador vs. Its Competitors

Total coffee export value (HS 0901), log scale — Ecuador is a minor player next to regional and global exporters.

Ecuador's raw coffee exports ($12–28M/yr) are a rounding error next to Brazil ($11.4B in 2024) and even Colombia ($3.5B) or Peru ($1.1B). Ecuador cannot compete on volume — its opportunity lies in value-added processing (instant coffee, specialty/roasted niches) rather than raw commodity export.
